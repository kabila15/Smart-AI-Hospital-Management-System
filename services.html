What We Offer

Hospital Services

Comprehensive care across all major medical specialties — all under one roof.

Medical Checkup

Comprehensive health screenings and routine checkups by experienced physicians to detect issues early and keep you in peak condition.

Cardiology

Advanced cardiac care including ECG, echocardiography, stress tests, and specialist consultation for heart conditions.

Nursing Services

Round-the-clock compassionate nursing care to support patient recovery, daily medical needs, and post-operative monitoring.

Pharmacy

In-house pharmacy stocked with all prescribed and over-the-counter medications, open seven days a week for your convenience.

Emergency Care

24/7 emergency department with rapid-response teams equipped to handle trauma, accidents, and critical medical situations.

Laboratory

Fully equipped diagnostic lab offering blood panels, pathology, microbiology, and imaging with fast, accurate results.

Gynaecology & Women's Health

Specialised care for women including prenatal services, gynaecological consultations, and maternal health programmes.

Neurology

Expert diagnosis and treatment for neurological conditions including stroke, epilepsy, migraines, and sleep disorders.

Paediatrics

Dedicated child-friendly care from newborns to adolescents, including vaccinations, growth monitoring, and specialist referrals.
